Tirzepatide monthly pricing protocol

One card for Square, the desk, and the website. Price the 4-week fill by weekly milligrams — never by milliliters, never by “how many months they’ve been here.”

Rules — no exceptions without the ordering clinician

  1. Highest dose in the fill wins. Four weekly injections = one month. If they go 7.5 → 10 during that fill, the whole month is $450. Do not split weeks.
  2. Hold the dose, hold the price. Six months at 5 mg is $399 every fill. Calendar month is not the key.
  3. Never quote milliliters. Say “5 milligrams weekly, $399 this month.” Vial size is pharmacy math, not the menu.
  4. Do not sell retired SKUs: 4-Week Tirzepatide $349 · Initial Consult + First Injection $349 · Monthly Maintenance $299 · Tirzepitide 10 week $600.
  5. 90-day prepay only when the dose is already stable. Do not prepay a titration. 3 × the band price (optional 10% off is owner-approved only).
  6. Front desk does not choose the dose. Schedule the consult. The clinician (Dr. Arora until an Illinois NP is credentialed) writes milligrams. Authorized RN injects the written order only.

Say this

“Your price is your weekly milligram dose for a 4-week fill. Month one at 2.5 is $299. If we go to 5 or 7.5, that month is $399. We only go to 10 or 12.5 if it is appropriate — those are $450 and $499.”

Do not say

  • “It’s $299 a month no matter the dose.”
  • “The 10-week program is $600.”
  • “We’ll just charge you for 5 mL.”
  • Outcome guarantees or “you will lose X pounds.”
