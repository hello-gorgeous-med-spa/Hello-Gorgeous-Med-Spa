How to hand them a file

  1. Open a sign in Chrome.
  2. Print → Save as PDF.
  3. Paper size 24 × 36 inches (portrait). Margins none. Background graphics on.
  4. Or run node scripts/export-washington-yard-signs.mjs from the repo — writes PDFs next to these HTML files.

QR codes already live in this folder at 1400px (high enough for a 3.4″ print square). Book QR → /book. Fall QR → /fall-makeover. Botox QR → /botox-oswego. Pout QR → /book (lips campaign). Weight-loss QR → /glp1-weight-loss. All tagged Washington Street.

What I would not print

  • A service list. Nobody reads Botox / Morpheus8 / HydraFacial from a car.
  • Client treatment photos. Faces of guests on a street sign is not worth it.
  • A giant photo of Danielle or Ryan full-bleed — those portraits are ~800px, they will look soft at 36″. Sign 3 keeps the photo in a frame on purpose.
  • Medical promises. No shade, pound, or wrinkle claims on Washington Street.
