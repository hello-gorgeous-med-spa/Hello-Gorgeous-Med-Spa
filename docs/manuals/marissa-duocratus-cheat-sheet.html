Golden rules: Start low → test spot → wait → treat. Endpoint = mild perifollicular redness / slight edema / warmth. No change → raise fluence 1–2 J/cm² and reassess. Pain / whitening / blister → STOP, lower energy, max cooling, escalate. Darker skin / thicker hair = lower energy, longer pulse, max cooling. Never treat over tattoos, moles, or pigmented lesions (stay ≥ 1.5 cm away). White / gray / very light hair = poor diode response — do not force high energy.

1 · 808 nm Diode — Laser hair removal (SAFE vs AGGRESSIVE)

Device range: fluence 1–120 J/cm² · pulse 5–400 ms · freq 1–10 Hz · cooling HIGH (head ~4°C before Ready). Area + Fitzpatrick on screen auto-presets — use table to sanity-check.

Fitzpatrick Menu cue SAFE start (first visit / test) AGGRESSIVE* (after proven tolerance) Pulse / cooling
I–II White / fair ~16–22 J/cm² ~24–30 J/cm² 10–30 ms · Cooling HIGH
III–IV Medium ~12–18 J/cm² ~20–24 J/cm² 20–40 ms · Cooling HIGH
V (VI = MD only) Dark ~10–12 J/cm² ~14–16 J/cm² 30–100 ms · Cooling MAX

*Aggressive = high end of HG conservative band for coarse/dense dark hair after safe response — not a max-out. Coarse back/legs tolerate higher; face/neck stay lower. Overall diode working band often 10–60 J/cm² clinically; never jump >2 J/cm² between passes.

2 · Diode treatment flow (every visit)

  1. Consent + screen + Fitzpatrick + hair color/type + photos
  2. Shave only (no wax/pluck 4 wks). Clean dry skin. Gel 2–3 mm for test/treat
  3. Goggles on everyone · room warning · standby when paused
  4. Select Laser HR → area → skin type → cooling HIGH
  5. Test spot discreet area · wait 5–10 min · observe
  6. Treat: tip flush, mild pressure, with hair growth · grid · ≤30% overlap · 1 pass unless MD says
  7. Wipe tip with 70% ethanol every 30–40 pulses
  8. Chart: area, Fitz, fluence, pulse, Hz, passes, response
  9. Cold gauze + soothing gel · aftercare · standby → wait 5 min → power down

4 · IPL filters — pick the right cut-off (DuoCratus)

IPL energy density typically 1–50 J/cm². Filters: 480 · 530 · 590 · 640 · 690. Use cooling gel. Test spot every new filter / new area.

Filter Use for SAFE start AGGRESSIVE* Notes
480 nm Acne / active pustules 10–12 J/cm² 13–15 J/cm² Porphyrins · Fitz I–III preferred
530 nm Sun spots, freckles, facial redness / small vessels 12–15 J/cm² 16–20 J/cm² Superficial pigment + hemoglobin · not melasma / not leg veins
590 nm Hyperpigmentation + photorejuvenation · rosacea / telangiectasia 14–18 J/cm² 19–24 J/cm² Hemoglobin & pigment · Fitz I–IV · pulse ~20–50 ms
640 nm Hair removal (light–medium skin) · some vascular 18–22 J/cm² 23–28 J/cm² Deeper melanin · Fitz I–IV
690 nm Rejuvenation depth / coarse dark hair · dense body hair 20–26 J/cm² 27–35 J/cm² Deep melanin · pulse often longer (~30–60 ms)

Marissa quick map: 590 = pigment / rejuvenation tone · 690 = deeper / coarse hair & deeper rejuvenation work. Prefer diode 808 for primary laser hair packages; use IPL 640/690 when protocol / MD directs.

5 · Pre-care (tell client)

  • No sun / tanning / self-tanner 4–8 weeks (longer if Fitz IV+)
  • Shave 12–24 hrs before — no wax/pluck 4 weeks
  • Stop retinoids / acids / bleaching agents on area ~14 days (screen)
  • No Roaccutane / oral retinoids prior 6 months without MD clearance
  • Clean skin — no lotion, makeup, deodorant

6 · After-care

  • Cool compress + soothing gel for redness/warmth
  • SPF 50+ · avoid sun on area ~1 month · no tanning
  • No hot showers, sauna, pool, heavy sweat 24–48 hrs
  • Don’t pick — shed hairs fall out over 1–2 weeks
  • Makeup only when skin is intact / not fragile
Hard stops / call Medical Director or Ryan FNP before treating: pregnancy / breastfeeding · pacemaker · metal/implants in area · active cancer · active herpes in area · open wounds · recent Accutane · fresh tan · tattoo in field · Fitz VI without written MD parameters · blistering on test spot · any concern outside training. Absolute vs relative contraindication list lives in the full Zemits diode manual — when in doubt, don’t treat.
