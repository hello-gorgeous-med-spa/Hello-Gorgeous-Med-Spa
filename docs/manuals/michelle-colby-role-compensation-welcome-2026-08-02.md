**2. Production commission — 20% after consumable cost (COGS)**  

**What “COGS” means:**  
**COGS = Cost of Goods Sold** — the real out-of-pocket cost of the consumable or product used to deliver that treatment (for example, a Morpheus8 needle tip). We subtract that cost from what the client paid **before** calculating your commission, so you’re paid on the practice’s true margin on the service — not on money that went straight to supplies.

**Formula:**  
**20% × (collected price − applicable COGS)**

On **collected** revenue for your five modality areas, when the sale/treatment is **attributed to you**:

| Modality | COGS (consumable cost) | Your commission base |
|---|---|---|
| Morpheus8 | $125 per needle / session | Price − $125 |
| Solaria CO₂ | $0 | Full collected price |
| FlowWave / shockwave | $0 | Full collected price |
| Laser hair removal | $0 | Full collected price |
| Weight loss / peptides | $75 midpoint* | Price − $75 |

\*Peptide / GLP-1 COGS will be refined to exact SKU costs; $75 is the working midpoint of ~$60–$90 until locked.

**Example — Morpheus8:** Client pays $799 · needle COGS $125 · net $674 · **your commission $134.80**.  
**Example — Solaria:** Client pays $899 · COGS $0 · **your commission $179.80**.

Multi-session packages deduct COGS for each session when the package naming includes a session count. Commission applies to **collected** revenue only (refunds, chargebacks, comps, and no-shows do not earn commission; any already-paid commission may be adjusted on a later pay period).

This structure **replaces** prior package/ReGen/FlowWave percentage stacks — one clear hourly rate + one clear net-of-COGS commission on your modalities.
