## The two packages (memorize these)

### Transformation · $2,400 — face hero

| Included | Why it matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **3× Morpheus8 Burst** | Deep RF microneedling (up to 8mm) — tighten, scars, texture, collagen |
| **1× Solaria CO₂ full face** | Fractional laser — surface renewal, tone, lines, sun damage |

**Who it’s for:** Face laxity + texture/tone in one plan. Client wants a “real glow-up,” not a one-and-done facial.

**À la carte reality check (use this):**

- 3× Morpheus8 face (~$800–$850 each) ≈ **$2,400–$2,550**
- 1× Solaria full face **$899**
- Separate booking ≈ **$3,300–$3,450+**
- Package **$2,400** → they save roughly **$900–$1,000+** and lock the series

### Ultimate · $3,000 — body hero

| Included | Why it matters |
|----------|----------------|
| **3× Morpheus8** on body area 1 | Abdomen, arms, thighs, knees, etc. |
| **3× Morpheus8** on body area 2 | Second zone in the same series |

**Who it’s for:** Post-weight-loss skin, crepey arms, abdomen + another zone, “I want my body to match my face.”

**À la carte reality check:**

- Body sessions list around **$1,200** each
- 6 body sessions separate ≈ **$7,200**
- Package **$3,000** — frame as series commitment pricing, not a random discount

## 90-second consult framework

1. **Mirror their goal** — “So the main thing is [jowls / acne scars / post-GLP skin / tone]…”
2. **Name the tech** — InMode verified · Morpheus8 Burst + Deep · Solaria CO₂ · NP-directed
3. **Explain why a series** — collagen builds over months; singles don’t finish the job
4. **Put the package on the table** — Transformation (face) or Ultimate (body)
5. **Show the math** — à la carte vs package
6. **Close the next step** — proposal link + deposit (or Cherry) today

That’s our **Transformation Package at $2,400** — three Morpheus8 plus Solaria, instead of paying for each à la carte around thirty-three hundred.”

> “Morpheus8 remodel**s under** the skin. Solaria refreshe**s the surface**. Doing only one leaves half the story unfinished for a lot of clients who want that full ‘I look rested and renewed’ result.”

### Money close

> “I’ll put Transformation / Ultimate on a **treatment proposal** with Essential, Recommended, and VIP options. You can open it on your phone, and we can take a **deposit today** on Square — or apply with **Cherry** if monthly payments feel better.”

| “I’ll think about it.” | “Perfect — I’ll send the **proposal link** so the numbers stay clear. If you want to hold the package pricing, a deposit locks your plan. No pressure — the link is yours.” |

## Timeline you can promise (process, not results)

- **Morpheus8 series:** typically **3 visits · ~4–6 weeks apart**
- Collagen remodeling continues **for months** after
- **Solaria:** plan **social downtime** at consult (often ~1 week for lighter plans; longer if deeper)
- Full Transformation calendar: map M8 visits + one Solaria week that fits their life

## Do / don’t

**Do**

- Use package names and round numbers ($2,400 / $3,000 / $899 / $1,999)
- Show à la carte math once
- Send a written proposal every time
- Pair downtime honesty with a calendar plan
- Upsell exosomes + vitamins after they say yes to the package

**Don’t**

- Guarantee “erase scars” / “look 10 years younger”
- Bash other med spas by name
- Undersell Solaria downtime
- Forget NP / InMode verified as the trust line
- Let them leave with only a verbal quote
