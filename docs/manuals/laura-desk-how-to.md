## Laura’s Desk — weekly workflow

### 1) Schedule meetings *before* they happen
In **Meetings & outreach**, add:
- Chamber / civic events  
- Meetings with businesses you’re marketing to  
- Outreach, follow-ups, content work you want tracked  

Fill in: title, org, category, date/time, location or Zoom, short goal note.

### 2) After each meeting — mark it done
Hit **Mark done** and write a short **outcome / next step**.  
If it didn’t happen → **Cancelled** or **No-show**.

### 3) Log your hours
When you work, log what you did + hours (goal **10 hrs/week**).

### 4) Monday checklist + invoice
Every Monday:
- Submit weekly plan + invoice  
- Check off accountability items (content calendar, posts, meetings logged, follow-ups, etc.)

### 5) Productivity toolkit (on Laura’s Desk)
Use the toolkit links for social, Google posts, Text Studio, email, templates, reviews, protocols kit, and blog→social — then log the work.

## Text Studio (SMS) — quick how-to

1. Marketing → **Text Studio** (or open https://www.hellogorgeousmedspa.com/admin/sms)
2. Confirm audience / opt-in rules and consent checkbox
3. Pick a template or write copy (watch segment count / cost)
4. Send
5. Log hours on Laura’s Desk (“SMS blast — [name of campaign]”)
6. If you made graphics, upload under **Resources**
