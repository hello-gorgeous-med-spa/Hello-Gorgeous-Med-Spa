## 1. Engagement; independent contractor status

1.1 **Engagement.** Practice engages Contractor, and Contractor accepts engagement, as an independent contractor to provide the Services in Section 2.

1.2 **Not employment.** Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, agency (except as expressly stated for HIPAA), franchise, or ownership interest. Contractor has **no ownership_status** in the Practice. Administrative or clinical access does not create ownership rights.

1.3 **Control of professional judgment.** Practice does not control the manner or means of Contractor’s licensed professional judgment. Practice may set facility rules, protocols, scheduling coordination, documentation standards, and brand/presentation standards. Clinical yes/no, dose, decline, and off-label decisions remain Contractor’s (or the covering licensed prescriber’s).

1.4 **No guaranteed hours or salary.** This is **pay-by-production**. There is no base salary, hourly clinical wage, monthly oversight stipend, retainer, or minimum draw. Practice does not guarantee a volume of patients, prescriptions, or collected revenue.

1.5 **Taxes.** Contractor is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, self-employment tax, and estimated payments. Practice will issue **Form 1099-NEC** as required. Contractor shall provide a current **W-9** before the first payout.

1.6 **Tools and expenses.** Practice provides the clinic premises, devices, EHR/POS access, and customary clinical supplies for in-spa work. Contractor is responsible for personal licensure, DEA (if applicable), national certification, CME, and professional liability premiums unless Practice agrees otherwise in a signed writing.

1.7 **Other work.** Contractor may maintain other professional activities that do not breach Sections 8–10 (confidentiality, non-solicit, IP) or create a patient-safety or brand conflict. Competing cash-pay peptide / GLP-1 / HRT telehealth prescribing for a direct competitor during the Term requires Practice’s prior written consent.

## 2. Scope of services

### 2.1 Esthetic / medical-aesthetic services (Hello Gorgeous Med Spa)

Contractor may personally perform esthetic and medical-aesthetic treatments at the Oswego location **within Illinois APRN scope, Practice protocols, device privileges, and Medical Director standing orders**, including without limitation:

- neuromodulators and dermal fillers;
- energy devices (including Morpheus8, QuantumRF, Solaria, laser / IPL, FlowWave / shockwave) for which Contractor is trained and privileged;
- IV therapy, PRP, and other in-clinic medical-aesthetic or wellness treatments assigned to Contractor;
- related good-faith exams, consents, charting, and complication management for treatments Contractor performs.

Contractor is **not** engaged as a licensed esthetician. Non-medical spa services remain with licensed esthetic staff unless a specific treatment is within Contractor’s license and assigned in writing.

### 2.2 Prescribing for RE GEN RX

Contractor is the Practice’s **prescribing clinician of record** for RE GEN RX / Hello Gorgeous RX cash-pay programs (weight loss / GLP-1, peptides, hormones, related compounded or branded medications, and associated consults) when Contractor is the treating / prescribing provider on the chart.

Prescribing Services include: chart review, good-faith exam (in person or compliant telehealth), medical-necessity determination, prescription or decline, titration / discontinue decisions, adverse-event response within protocol, and required documentation. **Nothing ships until Contractor (or a covering licensed prescriber) approves.**

Unlicensed staff (including the owner and front desk) **do not prescribe** and may not be paid a production split for writing or choosing a prescription.

### 2.3 Related duties (not separately paid)

Production pay in Section 3 covers, and is the sole compensation for, related professional duties: timely charting, consents, protocol adherence, collaboration with Dr. Arora as required by Illinois law and the collaborative / medical-director arrangement on file, reasonable coverage coordination, and cooperation with audits, inspections, pharmacy partners, and malpractice carriers.

### 2.4 No unauthorized services

Contractor shall not perform a service, use a device, or prescribe a molecule outside license, DEA authority, Practice formulary / protocols, or Medical Director standing orders.

## 3. Compensation — 20% pay-by-production

### 3.1 Rate

Practice shall pay Contractor a production commission equal to **twenty percent (20%)** of **Net Collected Production** attributable to Contractor, as defined below.

### 3.2 Two production buckets (same 20% rate; no stacking)

| Bucket | What earns 20% | Attribution rule |
|--------|----------------|------------------|
| **A — Services rendered** | Esthetic / medical-aesthetic services **personally performed** by Contractor | Square (or successor POS) team-member attribution to Contractor at checkout, or written owner override |
| **B — Prescriptions prescribed** | RE GEN RX / Hello Gorgeous RX program and medication revenue **collected** by Practice for a patient for whom Contractor **personally evaluated the chart and issued (or renewed) the prescription** as clinician of record | RE GEN sales attribution to Contractor as prescribing / treating clinician — not to the person who took the phone call or ran the card unless that person is also Contractor |

A single collected dollar is counted **once**. If a line could fall in both buckets, it is paid under the bucket that matches the catalog classification (RE GEN / peptide / GLP-1 / HRT lines → Bucket B; in-spa treatment lines → Bucket A).

### 3.3 Net Collected Production

**Net Collected Production** means amounts **actually received** by Practice from the patient (or the patient’s card / cash / approved financing) for eligible Bucket A or B items, minus:

- refunds, voids, returns, and chargebacks (including those after payout — clawed back from later periods);
- comps, no-shows, deposits that are not earned, and unpaid invoices;
- sales tax, tips, and **shipping / handling** (shipping is never commissioned);
- gift-card **sales** (commission is earned when the gift card is **redeemed** on an eligible service/Rx, not when the card is sold);
- third-party processing fees only if Practice later adopts a written net-of-fees policy; until then, commission is on collected price before card fees.

**Not commissioned:** retail product sold by others; another provider’s treatments; affiliate / spa-referral fees paid to third parties; pharmacy wholesale cost (commission is on **collected patient price**, not on COGS or profit); declined charts; consults that are not collected.

### 3.4 Consult fees

A collected NP / medical consult fee personally performed by Contractor is Bucket A (or Bucket B if the catalog classifies it as RE GEN). If the consult fee is **credited** toward a later program payment, it is not paid twice.

### 3.5 No quota; no pay for writing vs declining

Compensation is **not** a bounty per prescription written. Declining a prescription, stopping a medication, or choosing a lower-cost clinically appropriate option **does not** create a contractual default. Practice will **not** set a prescription quota, conversion quota, or “units written” target as a condition of this Agreement.

### 3.6 Pay cycle

Payouts are **weekly** (Monday–Sunday period, America/Chicago), via **Square Payroll contractor / 1099 payout** (or another method Practice designates in writing). Practice may preview amounts in the admin payroll tools. Payout is due after Practice has a reasonable opportunity to confirm collection and attribution for that period.

### 3.7 Attribution; disputes

Contractor must be tagged as the performing / prescribing provider in Square and/or the RE GEN attribution tools. Unattributed sales do not earn commission. Good-faith attribution disputes are decided by the owner, documented, and may be corrected on a later period.

### 3.8 Prior plan superseded

This Section 3 replaces in full the July 2026 structure (flat monthly oversight up to $1,500, 10/15/20% general-service tiers, 10% RE GEN, 50% Luxora, Google-review bonus). No stacking with any prior percentage.

## 4. Clinical independence and integrity (protects both Parties)

4.1 Contractor shall prescribe and treat **only** when, in Contractor’s independent professional judgment, the service or medication is appropriate for that patient. Marketing copy, production pay, and staff pressure do not authorize an unsafe or unnecessary order.

4.2 Practice may audit charts, standing-order compliance, and RE GEN prescribing patterns. Material unnecessary prescribing, documentation failure, or protocol breach is **cause** for immediate termination under Section 12.

4.3 This Agreement is intended as compensation for **personally performed professional services**, not as fee-splitting with an unlicensed person for the practice of medicine, not as a kickback for product selection, and not as a share of Practice profits or equity.

4.4 Cash-pay only under this Agreement. Contractor shall not bill federal health-care programs for these services unless Practice and Contractor execute a separate compliant arrangement.

---

## 5. Licensure, credentialing, and insurance

5.1 **Licenses.** Contractor shall maintain, at Contractor’s expense: unrestricted Illinois APRN license, national FNP-BC (or successor) certification, NPI, DEA if controlled substances will be prescribed, BLS, and any collaborative / practice agreement required by Illinois law with the Medical Director then serving the Practice.

5.2 **Notice.** Contractor shall notify Practice in writing within **24 hours** of any license restriction, DEA issue, malpractice claim, board complaint, exclusion from a federal program, or criminal charge.

5.3 **Professional liability.** Contractor shall maintain professional liability (malpractice) insurance covering aesthetic procedures and prescribing, with limits not less than **$1,000,000 per claim / $3,000,000 aggregate** (or the limits Practice’s carrier requires), naming **Hello Gorgeous, P.C.** as additional insured / certificate holder where the policy allows. Contractor shall deliver certificates on signing and on each renewal. Lapse is cause for immediate suspension of Services and pay.

5.4 **Privileges.** Device and injectable privileges are Practice’s to grant and revoke. Training certificates (e.g., InMode) shall be copied into Contractor’s credential file.

## 6. Patients, records, and practice property

6.1 **Patients belong to the Practice.** All patients, leads, charts, photos, treatment plans, pharmacy relationships, and contact lists are **Practice property**. Contractor is an assignee / treating clinician only. `primary_provider` status does not transfer ownership.

6.2 **Records.** All documentation is created and stored in Practice systems. Contractor shall not export, download, or copy the full patient list, photo library, or marketing list without owner-approved, logged export.

6.3 **Return.** On termination or on demand, Contractor shall immediately return badges, keys, devices, formulary sheets, and cease use of Practice accounts. Practice may disable access the same day.

6.4 **Name and likeness.** Practice may use Contractor’s name, credentials, and professional photos already approved for marketing. Contractor shall not use Practice marks, patient photos, or “Hello Gorgeous” / “RE GEN” branding for a competing venture.

## 7. HIPAA and confidential information

7.1 Contractor shall comply with HIPAA, HITECH, Illinois confidentiality law, and Practice privacy policies. PHI is used only to treat the patient and operate the Practice. Contractor shall execute the Practice’s Business Associate / confidentiality acknowledgment if requested.

7.2 **Confidential Information** includes (without limitation) patient data, wholesale and pharmacy pricing, compensation formulas, unreleased protocols, vendor terms, software, and non-public business plans. Contractor shall not disclose it during or after the Term except as required by law.

7.3 This Section 7 survives termination.

## 8. Non-solicitation (protects the book)

During the Term and for **eighteen (18) months** after it ends, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly:

- solicit, divert, or accept for a competing aesthetic, GLP-1, peptide, or HRT practice any patient Contractor treated or prescribed for at the Practice, using Practice confidential lists or knowledge;
- solicit Practice employees or contractors to leave;
- disparage the Practice, its owner, Medical Director, or staff in a manner that is false or intended to divert patients.

This Section does not bar a patient who independently seeks Contractor without solicitation, and does not bar truthful responses to a licensing board, court, or insurer. If a court finds the period or scope too broad, it shall be reformed to the maximum enforceable under Illinois law.

Contractor hereby assigns to Practice any work-product created in the course of the Services. Contractor retains Contractor’s pre-existing personal credentials and independently developed materials that do not contain Practice Confidential Information.

## 11. Indemnification; limitation

11.1 **Contractor** shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Practice, Danielle Alcala-Glazier, Hello Gorgeous, P.C., and their officers, staff, and Medical Director from claims arising out of Contractor’s professional negligence, unauthorized prescribing, scope violations, HIPAA breaches by Contractor, or Contractor’s misclassification-related tax claims **to the extent caused by Contractor**.

11.2 **Practice** shall indemnify Contractor for claims arising from Practice’s premises, devices it maintains, or staff conduct **not** directed by Contractor, except to the extent caused by Contractor.

11.3 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential damages, except for confidentiality, IP, non-solicit, or indemnification obligations, or willful misconduct.

## 12. Term and termination

12.1 **Term.** Starts on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

12.2 **Without cause.** Either Party may terminate on **fourteen (14) days’** written notice.

12.3 **For cause — immediate.** Practice may terminate immediately (and withhold unpaid commission pending audit) if Contractor: loses or restricts a required license; lets malpractice coverage lapse; commits fraud, theft, or HIPAA breach; engages in unnecessary or unsafe prescribing; solicits patients in breach of Section 8; represents themselves as owner/partner; or materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within **three (3) days** after notice where cure is reasonably possible.

12.4 **Final pay.** After termination, Practice pays earned Net Collected Production through the last day, minus clawbacks, within two regular pay cycles after attribution is closed. Unearned pipeline (unpaid invoices, unshipped orders later refunded) does not vest.

12.5 **Survival.** Sections 3.3 (clawbacks), 4, 6–11, 12.4, 13–16 survive.

13.1 Illinois law governs, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

13.2 Except for temporary injunctive relief to protect patients, PHI, IP, or the non-solicit, disputes shall be resolved by **binding arbitration** in **Kendall County, Illinois** (or DuPage County if Kendall is unavailable), before a single arbitrator experienced in healthcare business disputes, under AAA Commercial Rules. Each Party bears its own attorneys’ fees unless the arbitrator awards fees for a willful breach of Sections 6–8.

13.3 Class or representative actions are waived to the extent permitted by law.
