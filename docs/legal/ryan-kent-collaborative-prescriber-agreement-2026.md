## 1. At-will independent contractor

1.1 **At-will.** This engagement is **at will**. Either Party may end it at any time, for any reason or no reason, by written notice (email to **provider@hellogorgeousmedspa.com** or Contractor’s notice email is enough). No minimum term.

1.2 **Not employment.** Nothing here creates employment, partnership, joint venture, equity, or ownership. Administrative or clinical access does not create ownership rights.

1.3 **No guaranteed hours or salary.** Practice will make clinic time and **online-bookable telehealth** available as the schedule allows. Practice does not guarantee full-time hours, a volume of patients, or collected revenue. Contractor may accept other professional work that does not breach Sections 8–10 or create a patient-safety or brand conflict.

1.4 **Taxes.** Contractor pays all federal, state, and local taxes and estimated payments. Practice issues **Form 1099-NEC**. Current **W-9** before the first payout.

1.5 **Wind-down.** After notice, Contractor remains reasonably available for **seven (7) days** for urgent questions on open prescriptions and in-progress charts. Access may be turned off the same day if ended for cause (Section 12).

## 2. Role — clinical lead, RE GEN RX

2.1 Contractor is the Practice’s **clinical lead for RE GEN RX** / Hello Gorgeous RX: peptides, weight loss / GLP-1, related cash-pay programs, telehealth, and the written protocols staff follow.

2.2 Lead means: adopt and update protocols, set the telehealth / consult standard, be the prescribing clinician of record, train designated staff on the protocol pathway, and remain reachable for out-of-protocol questions. Lead is **not** ownership, partnership, or control of Practice bank accounts, hiring, or brand.

2.3 Contractor may also personally perform in-clinic medical-aesthetic services within Illinois APRN scope, device privileges, and Medical Director standing orders, including **scheduled Quantum RF**, injectables, consults, and other treatments assigned to his book.

## 3. Protocol prescribing — license, charting, call-ins

3.1 **Ryan remains the prescriber.** Contractor adopts written clinical protocols for RE GEN RX medications and related orders. Those protocols are how the Practice may act **on his license and under his authority**. Designated trained staff do **not** become prescribers. They do not choose a drug or dose outside the protocol.

3.2 **What staff may do under protocol.** When a patient meets the written protocol criteria, designated staff may:

- complete intake and **chart** in Practice systems;
- prepare the order that matches the protocol;
- **call in / transmit** the prescription to the pharmacy;
- contact Contractor when needed.

3.3 **When they must call Ryan.** Staff shall stop and reach Contractor (phone / text / Practice channel) before transmitting if: the case is **outside protocol**; first-time or high-risk; the patient is a minor (not a default program); adverse event or red-flag history; the pharmacy questions the order; or staff are unsure. **Nothing ships that is outside protocol until Contractor (or a covering licensed prescriber) is reached and approves.**

3.4 **Availability.** Contractor shall publish reasonable coverage hours and a reachable number for protocol questions. Practice will not represent the owner or unlicensed staff as the prescriber.

3.5 **No quota.** Production pay is not a bounty per prescription. Declining, stopping, or choosing a lower-cost appropriate option is not a breach.

## 4. Compensation

Paid weekly via Square Payroll 1099 (or another method Practice designates). Tag Ryan as performing / prescribing provider. Unattributed sales pay $0. One collected dollar is paid under **one** bucket only.

### 4.1 Bucket Q — Quantum (50% NET)

For **scheduled Quantum RF / Luxora** services **Contractor personally performs**, Practice pays **fifty percent (50%) of NET**.

**NET** means amounts actually collected for that Quantum service, minus refunds, voids, chargebacks, comps, no-shows, unpaid invoices, sales tax, tips, shipping, and **applicable consumable / tip / cartridge COGS** for that session.

### 4.2 Bucket R — All other professional production (20% after cost)

For the following, when **collected** and attributed to Contractor, Practice pays **twenty percent (20%) after cost**:

- peptides and weight-loss / RE GEN programs he **prescribes** (including protocol-pathway orders under Section 3, when he is clinician of record);
- consulting appointments he performs;
- **telehealth** visits he performs (including visits booked online);
- injectables he performs;
- **any other services he personally performs** that are not Bucket Q.

**After cost** means collected patient price minus refunds / comps / unpaid amounts minus **applicable COGS** (pharmacy / wholesale / program cost, or injectable consumable). Shipping is never commissioned. Gift-card **sales** are not commissioned; redemption on an eligible line is.

### 4.3 Worked examples (illustrative)

- Scheduled Quantum session collected $3,900, consumable COGS $400, performed by Ryan → NET $3,500 → commission **$1,750**.
- Telehealth visit collected $99, COGS $0, Ryan performed → **$19.80**.
- Tirzepatide month collected $399, pharmacy cost $120, Ryan prescribed (or protocol pathway under his name) → after cost $279 → commission **$55.80**.
- Toxin visit collected $400, performed by Ryan, COGS $0 → **$80**.
- Chart declined / nothing collected → **$0**.

### 4.4 Clawback

Refunds and chargebacks after payout deduct from later periods.

### 4.5 Prior plans

This Section 4 replaces the September 11, 2026 flat 20% production agreement and the July 2026 mix. No stacking.

## 6. Clinical independence

Contractor treats and prescribes only when appropriate in his independent judgment. Marketing and production pay do not authorize an unsafe order. Practice may audit charts and protocol compliance. Material unsafe prescribing, documentation failure, or protocol breach is cause for immediate end of the engagement.

This is compensation for professional services — not fee-splitting with an unlicensed person for the practice of medicine, not a kickback for product selection, and not equity.

Cash-pay only unless the Parties sign a separate compliant arrangement.

## 9. Non-solicitation

During the engagement and for **eighteen (18) months** after, Contractor shall not solicit Practice patients he treated or prescribed for, or Practice staff, for a competing aesthetic, GLP-1, peptide, or HRT practice using Practice lists or knowledge. A patient who independently seeks him without solicitation is not a breach. A court may reform this clause to the maximum enforceable under Illinois law.

## 12. Ending the engagement

12.1 **At-will.** Either Party, any time, written notice.

12.2 **For cause — immediate.** License loss or restriction; malpractice lapse; fraud, theft, or HIPAA breach; unnecessary or unsafe prescribing; patient solicitation in breach of Section 9; representing himself as owner/partner.

12.3 **Final pay.** Earned NET / after-cost production through the last day, minus clawbacks, within two regular pay cycles after attribution closes.
