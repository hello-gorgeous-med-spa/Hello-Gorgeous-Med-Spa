## Purpose

This binder is the practice’s **written clinical and operations packet** for day-to-day care and for **Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR)** inspection readiness.

Keep the **signed original** in a labeled 3-ring binder at the **front desk**. Staff must be able to produce it the same day a surveyor, pharmacy partner, or carrier asks. A reprint lives at Admin → Clinical Guidance → Compliance Binder.

These documents are **practice templates**. They are not a substitute for the Medical Practice Act, the Nurse Practice Act, current IDFPR/IDPH med-spa guidance, pharmacy contracts, or advice from Illinois healthcare counsel. Completing this form does not certify compliance.
