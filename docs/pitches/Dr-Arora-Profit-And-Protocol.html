Prepared for Dr. Mukesh Arora, MD · Hello Gorgeous Med Spa / RE GEN · Companion to the partnership summary

Profit is not five spas injecting peptides. Profit is one clinic, one NP, many front doors — paid when medication is paid, then kept on a refill cadence Ryan already runs.

Where the money comes from

The patient pays Hello Gorgeous. We pay pharmacy cost, Ryan (already on payroll), you, and the referring spa. The spa is paid once. You are paid a retainer plus a small acquisition override. Month two and after is where the practice gets healthy — that is by design.

Why this is profitable for both of us

You

Fixed $1,500 for a practice that already has an NP. $500 because your name opens five doors. $25 only when a door actually converts. $250 when you show up for kickoff. You do not buy inventory or read 40 charts.

Your spas

$100 cash when the first vial is paid. No fridge, no pharmacy license, no residual accounting. They keep facials and lashes. Peptides stop being “we should offer that” and become a card at the desk.

Hello Gorgeous

We already built intake, consult, Ryan’s queue, pharmacy, portal, refill SMS. Your network fills that queue. We eat month-1 referral cost to own month-2+ revenue and the medical relationship.

Exact business protocol

  1. Intro. You introduce Danielle to the owner/manager of spa #1. You are the reason they take the meeting.
  2. One-page referral agreement. Danielle papers it. They may hand our QR. They may not store, sell, or inject medication. Illinois patients only.
  3. Kickoff (you, 30 minutes, $250). You sit with their front desk: script, QR tent card, “you are sending them to Hello Gorgeous — we are the clinic.”
  4. Door live. Danielle marks that spa live. Your $500 / month network retainer starts with the first live door.
  5. Scan. Client scans. Cookie attributes them to that spa for 90 days. They are now a Hello Gorgeous / RE GEN patient.
  6. $49 NP consult. Intake is free to submit. Consult fee holds the visit. Medication is not billed yet.
  7. Ryan reviews within one business day. Yes / no / change. Ugly charts (cardiac history, odd labs, a deny he is unsure about) escalate to you the same day.
  8. First medication paid. Patient pays Hello Gorgeous (Square). Pharmacy fills. Pickup in Oswego or ship. System queues $100 to the spa and $25 to you. One time per patient, lifetime.
  9. Danielle pays the ledger. Monthly: spa cuts, your overrides, retainers on the 1st, kickoffs when you sat in. W-9. Not equity.
  10. Repeat doors 2–5 the same way. No five-spa dump in week one. Spa #1 clean, then the next intro.

90-day scoreboard (so we know it is working)

We count

Doors live. Scans. Consults paid. First meds paid. Refill rate at day 35 and day 70. Escalations to you. Charts you sampled. Payouts marked paid.

We do not count
