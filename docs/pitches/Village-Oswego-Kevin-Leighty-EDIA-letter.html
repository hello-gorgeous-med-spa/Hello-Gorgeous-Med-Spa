Waubonsee is our community college. I am proud to be there. Finishing my prereqs at a 3.8 while running a downtown medical spa was not easy. Starting clinicals on January 1st is a promise I made to myself and to every client who has trusted me since 2017: I will not stop at “good enough.” I will become the kind of clinician this town can count on for the long haul. Ryan and Dr. Arora are the licensed medical leadership of the practice today. I am the owner who is going back to school so the next chapter of Hello Gorgeous is even more rooted in Oswego.

What I have already put into this street

I invested approximately $500,000 in professional, medical-grade InMode technology for this location, including Class IV laser platforms. That is not décor. That is hospital-grade equipment in a downtown storefront, so a mom from Oswego does not have to drive to Naperville for care we can give her here.

Ryan, Dr. Arora, and I are collaborating to bring real medical oversight to this block — seven-day NP coverage and a physician on our staff. The cost of doing that legally, with protocols, insurance, inventory, and professional payroll, is heavy. I feel it every month. I am still choosing it, because my clients deserve a practice that is top-notch, not a shortcut.

The storefront I am asking you to help me finish

The medicine inside has outgrown the face of the building. I need professional exterior signage and a storefront that looks like the medical practice we actually are — and an interior remodeled into an elegant, professional clinical setting. New furniture. Inventory I have to keep on hand because we are a medical practice, not a gift shop. Marketing dollars so the people I have served since 2017 still know we are here, and so new neighbors can find us.

I will be honest with you, because this letter is from my heart: new professional competition opened next door, and with the overhead of new staff and physician oversight, there are nights I am scared that everything I built might not be enough. I am not writing to ask the Village to pick a side on the block. I am writing because I want to stay. I want to stay loyal to Oswego. I want to stay loyal to the clients who have walked through my door since 2017. I would rather invest in this street than give up on it.
