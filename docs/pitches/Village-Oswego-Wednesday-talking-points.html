Script

Open. Thank you for making time on a Wednesday. I know you have a lot of downtown requests. I did not come to ask the Village to invent a business. Hello Gorgeous has been on Washington Street since 2017. I came to show you what I have already put into this building, and to ask for help finishing the part the street sees.

1. Dr. Arora — $1,500 a month. I hired Dr. Mukesh Arora, MD, to provide physician oversight. His rate is $1,500 a month. That is $18,000 a year of medical authority on this block, and it comes out of my pocket every month. I am not asking the grant to pay Dr. Arora. I am telling you so you know this is a medical practice with a real doctor.

2. Ryan Kent — family NP, seven days, sports physicals. Ryan Kent is a family nurse practitioner. He is on site seven days a week. He takes care of our clients here — including sports physicals — so a parent in Oswego does not have to leave town. That is a quality-of-life amenity in the TIF district. I pay for that coverage. You get the access downtown.

3. The med room. We installed plumbing so we can have a sink. Labor, cabinet, water — I paid for it. Interior medical buildout is mine. I am not putting that on the grant.

4. Paint and furniture. Over $1,000 in paint and supplies, about $5,000 in furniture. Again — mine.

5. InMode ~$500,000. Destination technology on Washington Street. The Village should not buy my lasers. The Village should help the outside catch up to what is already inside.

6. Front desk manager. A downtown job and a professional door for people walking in off the street.

7. Laura Witt — Chamber and events. Marketing manager so we are part of this community — not a locked door with a pretty logo.

The turn. Your program is for downtown TIF businesses that improve quality and character, and that show amenities and jobs. I have already delivered the medicine, the jobs, and the interior. What is still unfinished is the storefront: professional signage, doors and windows, exterior paint and masonry, lighting on the facade. I am asking for the full $40,000 for that work only. For every dollar you put on the face of this building, I have already put more than twelve dollars of InMode equipment behind the glass.

Close. I would rather invest in this street than give up on it. Please consider us for the full $40,000, enroll us in Business Concierge, and tell me the next piece of paper you need. Then stop talking. Let them answer.

If they push back

“This sounds like interior / payroll.” Correct. That is why I listed it as already paid. The application is exterior signage and storefront only.

“Do you have bids?” Not in this folder yet. Tell me the format you need and I will get three bids for signage and facade.

“Other businesses got $40k plus a loan.” Today the ask is the $40,000 storefront award. I will listen if a revolving loan is on the table later.

“Is medical even downtown retail?” It is a highly desired amenity. Families should not have to leave town for a physical or for this care.

Do not talk about the competitor next door. Stay loyal to Oswego.
