## Script you can almost read

**Open**

Thank you for making time on a Wednesday. I know you have a lot of downtown requests. I did not come to ask the Village to invent a business. Hello Gorgeous has been on Washington Street since 2017. I came to show you what I have already put into this building, and to ask for help finishing the part the street sees.

**1. Dr. Arora — $1,500 a month**

I hired Dr. Mukesh Arora, MD, to provide physician oversight. His rate is $1,500 a month. That is $18,000 a year of medical authority on this block, and it comes out of my pocket every month. I am not asking the grant to pay Dr. Arora. I am telling you so you know this is a medical practice with a real doctor, not a retail spa that borrowed a name.

**2. Ryan Kent — family NP, seven days, sports physicals**

Ryan Kent is a family nurse practitioner. He is on site seven days a week in our downtown location. He takes care of our clients here — including sports physicals — so a parent in Oswego does not have to leave town for something that simple. That is a quality-of-life amenity in the TIF district. I pay for that coverage. You get the access downtown.

**3. The med room — plumbing, sink, cabinet, water**

We installed plumbing in the med room so we can have a sink. Labor, cabinet, water — I paid for it. That is how you know we are serious: we put a clinical wet room in a downtown storefront. I am not putting that on the grant. Interior medical buildout is mine.

**4. Paint and furniture**

We spent over $1,000 in paint and supplies, and about $5,000 in furniture. Again — mine. I wanted the inside to feel like the medicine we already bought.

**5. Half a million in InMode**

I invested about $500,000 in InMode equipment in this building. That is the largest check. That is destination technology on Washington Street. The Village should not buy my lasers. The Village should help the outside of the building catch up to what is already inside it.

**6. Front desk manager**

I hired a front desk manager. That is a downtown job and a professional door for people walking in off the street.

**7. Laura Witt — Chamber and events**

I hired Laura Witt as marketing manager specifically to get us more involved in the Chamber and to do events so we are part of this community — not a locked door with a pretty logo. If you want downtown to feel alive, that is the kind of payroll I am choosing.

**The turn — justify the $40,000**

Your program is for businesses in the downtown TIF that improve the quality and character of downtown, and that show amenities and jobs that might not otherwise get finished. I have already delivered the medicine, the jobs, and the interior. What is still unfinished is the storefront: professional signage, doors and windows, exterior paint and masonry, lighting on the facade.

Forty thousand dollars is the maximum Economic Incentive Award. I am asking for the full amount for **that** work only. For every dollar you put on the face of this building, I have already put more than twelve dollars of InMode equipment behind the glass.

I will bring contractor bids. I will go through Business Concierge. I will follow your eligibility list. I am not going to ask you to reimburse furniture or payroll — I know that is not what this award is for.

**Close**

I would rather invest in this street than give up on it. Please consider us for the full $40,000, enroll us in Business Concierge, and tell me the next piece of paper you need. I will do the homework.

Then stop talking. Let them answer.

## If they push back

**“This sounds like interior / payroll.”**  
Correct. That is why I listed it as **already paid**. The application I want to file is exterior signage and storefront only.

**“Do you have bids?”**  
Not in this folder yet. I wanted you to see the investment first. Tell me the format you need and I will get three bids for signage and facade.

**“Other businesses got $40k plus a loan.”**  
I am here for the Economic Incentive Award. If a revolving loan is also on the table later, I will listen. Today the ask is the $40,000 storefront award.

**“Sports physicals / medical — is that even downtown retail?”**  
It is a highly desired amenity. Families already shop and eat downtown. They should not have to leave town for a physical or for medical-spa care. That is exactly the quality-of-life test on your incentives page.

**Do not** talk about the competitor next door. Do not ask them to pick a side. Stay loyal to Oswego.
