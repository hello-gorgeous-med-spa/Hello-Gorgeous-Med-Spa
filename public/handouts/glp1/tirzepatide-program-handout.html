Your Tirzepatide Program

Medically supervised weight-loss care with Ryan Kent, FNP-BC. This sheet explains what you booked, what you receive, and how pricing works — so every visit matches the right service.
Important — please read before medication is dispensed

The service you book online is the service you are charged for — and it sets the maximum medication volume you can receive that visit. Monthly Maintenance ($299) is not a high-volume fill. If your prescribed supply is 5–10 ml per injection (or higher), staff must switch the visit to the matching volume service and charge that price before medication leaves the room.

How the program works
Step 1
Consult
  • Health history & goals review
  • Eligibility screening
  • Custom weekly dose plan
Step 2
Start
  • First injection or take-home supply
  • Injection training if needed
  • Titration schedule explained
Step 3
Maintain
  • Monthly follow-up & refill
  • Dose adjusted only by provider
  • Price follows your volume tier

What’s included in the program

  • Provider-guided dosing & adjustments
  • Medication for the tier you paid for
  • Injection education when needed
  • Ongoing check-ins & support

What this is not

  • Not a guarantee of a specific weight result
  • Not permission to change your own dose
  • Not a “grab any volume for $299” refill
  • Not a substitute for nutrition & strength work

Brand names Zepbound® / Mounjaro® may appear on menus for recognition. Your plan may use a compounded tirzepatide product when clinically appropriate. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved drug products for all uses described. Pricing reflects current clinic menu and may update.

What to expect between visits

At home

  • Inject only the dose your provider prescribed
  • Rotate sites (belly, thigh, upper arm)
  • Store medication as instructed
  • Prioritize protein + strength training

Call us right away for

  • Persistent vomiting or can’t keep fluids down
  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Signs of dehydration or allergic reaction
  • Questions before changing anything yourself
