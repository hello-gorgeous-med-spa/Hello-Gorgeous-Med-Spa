The questions everyone asks

What is Solaria CO₂?+
Solaria is InMode’s fractional ablative CO₂ laser. Tiny columns of treatment, with untreated skin in between so you heal faster than old full-field CO₂. It is a medical resurfacing treatment — not a lunchtime facial.
What does it treat?+
Fine lines, sun damage and brown spots, texture, pores, acne-scar appearance, lip lines, and mild laxity. Neck and chest can be treated more gently than the face. We plan from the photo you dislike — not a package name.
How long is downtime?+
Light: redness ~2–3 days, makeup often 5–7. Moderate: redness 3–5 days, makeup ~7–10. Deeper: redness 5–7 days, makeup 2+ weeks. If you have an event in 10 days, we do not do moderate or deep. We match depth to your calendar.
When will I see results?+
Week one is peel and pink. Glow and texture keep changing as you heal. Collagen can remodel for about 3–6 months. Results vary. We do not promise a magazine photo.
How many treatments?+
Some people love one well-planned treatment. Deep scars or heavy sun may need a series months apart. Session count depends on area, how you heal, and downtime you can take — decided at consult, not from a flyer.
Does it hurt?+
We numb 30–45 minutes (not more than about 45–60). Most feel heat and mild prickling, then a sunburn after. Sensitive skin gets extra caution. Any reaction to numbing — we stop.
Solaria vs Morpheus8?+
Solaria resets the surface (tone, etch, sun, texture). Morpheus8 works under the surface (collagen, tightening). Different tools. Some people need one. Some need both. We pick from the photo, not a trend.

Neck or chest?+
Yes — gentler than the face on purpose. Off-face skin needs reduced settings. That is safety, not an upsell. Those zones are quoted separately.
Under-eyes?+
Possible with a small-area tip and a lighter plan if you are a candidate. Eye shields when we treat near the eyes. Mapped at consult — not a walk-in add-on.
When can I wear makeup?+
When the surface has started to re-heal and we clear you — often 5–7 days light, 7–10 moderate, 2+ weeks deeper. Do not pack makeup into peeling skin.
Gym / sweat / sauna?+
Not until the skin is closed and we say so. Sweat in open laser columns is an infection and irritation risk. We will give you a date based on your depth.
Sun?+
No fresh tan or beds before. After: SPF 50+ and a hat for the first weeks. Sun on healing CO₂ is how people stain. Beach trip coming? We wait.
I get cold sores.+
Tell us. Laser around the mouth can wake a cold sore. Many people start an antiviral 1–2 days before. Don’t hide it — pretreat is easier than an outbreak on peeling skin.
I’m on Accutane / isotretinoin.+
Recent isotretinoin is a pause. Healing risk can stay elevated after a course. We screen this and wait until it is appropriate. Don’t stop a prescription on your own.
Is it OK on darker skin?+
CO₂ can change pigment on any skin — higher risk of darkening on deeper tones. We type your skin, talk through risk, and may test-spot or choose a different plan. We do not use one depth for everyone.
I have melasma.+
CO₂ is not automatically the first tool for melasma — heat can flare it. We look at your pattern and sun habits. Honesty here can save you a worse blotch.
I still have active acne.+
Open or very active breakouts may mean we wait. CO₂ is for texture and scars after the fire is out — not a substitute for an acne plan.
Pregnant or breastfeeding?+
We do not perform Solaria during pregnancy. Breastfeeding is a clinician call at consult. Trying to conceive? Tell us so we can time numbing, antivirals, and downtime.
I have Botox / filler.+
Bring dates and areas. Many people keep neuromodulators and wait a period after filler before aggressive laser on that zone. We will not guess.
Will I look scary / peel in sheets?+
Red, then bronzy, then flake. Deeper plans look more dramatic. That is expected. Do not pick — the bronzy layer is part of healing. Ice, ointment, extra pillows as we show you.
Do you test-spot?+
When it helps us choose settings — especially for skin type or a setting change. A tiny test can save a full-face surprise.
Same day as consult?+
Only if you are a candidate, you can take the downtime, prep is done (including antiviral if needed), and the schedule allows. Many people consult first and book a date that matches work and events.
How long do results last?+
You keep aging and the sun keeps working. A strong treatment can change the baseline; SPF and habits decide how long it looks that way. We do not sell “permanent.”
Who should not have this?+
We screen pregnancy, recent isotretinoin, active infection, certain healing problems, event timelines you cannot protect, and anyone who cannot do aftercare or sun avoidance. Final yes/no is clinical.
