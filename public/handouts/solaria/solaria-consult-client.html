Visit

What today looks like

▸ Free consult first

We check your history, skin type, goals, and how much social downtime you can take. If CO₂ is not the right tool, we say so.

▸ Comfort plan

Topical numbing is typically 30–45 minutes. Sensitive skin gets extra care. If anything feels wrong, we stop.

▸ Treatment

The laser is customized to your face and — if we add it — neck. Mild plans are quicker; deeper plans take longer. Most visits are 1½–3 hours with photos, numbing, and aftercare.

Before

How to arrive ready

  • No fresh tan, tanning beds, or unprotected sun.
  • Come with clean, product-free skin — no lotion, makeup, perfume, or oils.
  • A button-down shirt is easier after face / neck / chest.
  • No contact lenses in the treatment area visit.
  • If we use stronger comfort measures, arrange a ride home.
  • We may start an antiviral the day or two before if your history calls for it.
After

The first week at home

  • Cool packs as directed (on/off — do not freeze the skin).
  • Keep ointment on as we show you. Do not pick or scrub scabs.
  • Gentle cleanser twice a day once we say you can wash.
  • Sleep with extra pillows if the face is puffy.
  • Broad-spectrum SPF 50+ and a hat. No sun-seeking for the first weeks.
  • Itching and tightness are common. Call us for anything that worries you.
