Why we use carbon dioxide

Skin is mostly water. CO₂ light is grabbed by that water, so it can vaporize a precise column of tissue — then the untreated skin beside it heals the gap. That is why this is a medical procedure, not a facial.

The simple version

A grid of tiny “reset” columns

Solaria does not sand the whole face off. It lays thousands of microscopic columns. Each column: damaged cells gone. Between columns: healthy bridges that grow new skin in. You peel because the old surface is leaving. You keep improving for months because collagen underneath is remodeling.

Resurfacing

New surface

▸ What it is

Taking off sun-worn, etched, dull top layers in those columns so a new epidermis can form.

▸ Why CO₂

Creams and facials cannot vaporize a controlled depth of skin. Non-ablative lasers heat; they do not open the surface this way. CO₂ is chosen when the job is a true reset.

Texture

Peaks and valleys

▸ What it is

Pores, roughness, acne-scar look — architecture you can feel. Ablation knocks down peaks. Heat around each column tells the skin to build collagen in the valleys.

▸ Why more energy than a color pass

Texture lives deeper than a sun spot. That is why the texture program is not the same as a pigment pass — and why downtime has to match.

Why medical

We are creating a planned wound so it can heal better

If it did not injure the skin in a controlled way, it would not rebuild it. That is also why we screen you, protect your eyes, use a smoke evacuator, map downtime, and send you home with ointment and SPF — not a “no downtime glow.”

Your result is yours. Collagen keeps working about 3–6 months. We do not promise a magazine photo.
