Consent

A. General consent for treatment

I authorize Hello Gorgeous Med Spa and its licensed providers to perform medical aesthetic and wellness treatments discussed in my consultation. I understand medicine is not exact science and no results are guaranteed. Risks may include pain, swelling, bruising, infection, allergic reaction, scarring, asymmetry, unsatisfactory results, and need for additional treatment. Alternatives (including no treatment) have been discussed. I may withdraw consent before treatment.

B. HIPAA / privacy

I acknowledge Hello Gorgeous may use and disclose my protected health information for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations as allowed by HIPAA. I may request a copy of the Notice of Privacy Practices. I will not photograph other patients or discuss others’ care.

C. Medical weight loss / GLP-1 / peptide consent (if applicable)

Medications may include: Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide, and related compounded or brand products when prescribed.

  • These medications affect appetite, gastric emptying, and blood sugar; weekly subcutaneous injection is typical.
  • Common effects: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, stomach pain, decreased appetite.
  • Serious risks (less common): pancreatitis, gallbladder issues, kidney problems, allergic reaction, thyroid tumors (boxed warning in animal studies).
  • Do not use with personal/family MTC or MEN 2, or if pregnant / breastfeeding / trying to conceive without provider clearance.
  • Compounded versions are not FDA-approved drug products for all described uses; off-label use may apply.
  • I will report severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, or concerning symptoms immediately and attend follow-ups.
  • Dose changes are provider-directed only. Weight may return if medication is stopped.

D. Photo / before & after (optional but recommended)

E. Financial / charging accuracy

I understand charges are based on the service booked and the medication volume/dose dispensed. If volume increases, the visit may be rebooked/recharged to the correct tier before I leave. I am responsible for amounts not covered by any third party.

Your Before & After Guide

Keep this page · Hello Gorgeous Med Spa · (630) 636-6193

Before your visit / dose

  • Eat a lighter meal if injections make you nauseated
  • Bring a list of current meds & allergies
  • Wear clothes that allow injection-site access
  • Arrive with intake/consents complete
  • Ask every question — no guessing on dose
Optional: tape baseline photo here

After your visit / dose

  • Inject only the prescribed dose
  • Rotate sites (belly, thigh, upper arm)
  • Refrigerate medication as instructed
  • Prioritize protein + water + strength training
  • Track how you feel for your follow-up
Optional: tape progress photo here

What “progress” looks like (honest expectations)

Often improves

  • Food noise / portion control
  • Steady weekly routine
  • Provider-guided titration

Not guaranteed

  • A specific number on the scale
  • Zero side effects
  • Results without nutrition & muscle work

  • Vomiting that won’t stop / can’t keep fluids down
