1. Who we are

2. Your hours and your book

  • You commit to at least 25 hours a week.
  • Every other Saturday is required. Danielle will give you the Saturday rotation in writing.
  • Weekday hours are set with Danielle — some mornings, some afternoons, some evenings — so we stay open for real client availability.
  • You will have Square online / staff access. You may open extra hours you want to work. Our goal is to get you busy; the book is also yours to grow.
  • Be ready 10 minutes before your first client. Reset the room before the next walk-in.
  • Text or call Danielle if you will be late or out. A no-show on your side without notice can end the engagement.
  • After a 14-day ramp, repeating empty blocks may be shortened or removed with 7 days’ notice. Hourly pay is for a working shift.
  • Time off is granted with 5 days’ notice to Danielle at provider@hellogorgeousmedspa.com. If you are sick or in an emergency, tell her as soon as you can.

If a treatment needs more time than the slot, book a second slot and charge for the work. Do not run over into the next client’s time.

5. Training path

We will train you on site (paid at $50/hour):

  • Laser treatments
  • Solaria CO₂
  • Morpheus8
  • Microneedling
  • Advanced techniques: PDRN, baby Botox, exosomes

You treat paying clients on a modality only after you are trained and Danielle (and, where required, the medical director) signs you off. If you are unsure, you stop and ask.

6. Clinical & safety

  • Consent signed before you treat. Chart the same day. Photos as we use them.
  • You work under medical-director protocols. Vascular emergency or unexpected reaction: stop, follow protocol, call the covering provider. Do not “wait and see.”
  • Illinois RN license and BLS stay current. Malpractice covering cosmetic injectables stays current. Certificates on file before the first paid face.
  • You do not prescribe. You do not change a written dose. If a RE GEN / GLP-1 visit needs a new order, the NP or MD writes it.

7. HIPAA, phones, and photos

  • No client faces on your personal camera roll “for fun.”
  • No names in group chats or Instagram DMs.
  • No posting a client without a signed media release.
  • Lock the screen. Do not discuss one client in front of another.

8. Look and conduct

  • Clean, polished, on-brand. Hair off the face when treating. Nails safe for glove work.
  • No arguing prices at the front. No talking down the last injector. No drama on the floor.
  • Phone: clients first. Personal scrolling between faces, not in front of them.

9. Product and pricing

Published menu or owner-approved quote. Units used = units charged. Unbilled product can come off your next deposit at retail. Written owner approval is the only exception, and it happens before the needle.

10. Clients and loyalty

Clients, charts, before/afters, and price lists stay here. You do not take our book with you. During this engagement you do not inject, consult, or book for another med spa or aesthetic telehealth brand during Hello Gorgeous scheduled hours. Ask in writing if you think something is a gray area.

11. How you get paid

Square team access + your bank on file. Hours, production hours, tips, and 10% commission are approved by Danielle. Unbilled-product deductions come off the same deposit.
