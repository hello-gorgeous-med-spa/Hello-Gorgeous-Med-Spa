REGEN RX operating system — A to Z

How Danielle and Ryan run online orders: the website, automatic Bluefin pay links, Charm as backup, and Formulation / FormuConnect. Print this. Teach from this. Do not invent a third payment system.

Ryan writes the Rx. Danielle or Ryan send it to Formulation. Patients never order from a pharmacy. Stripe is off.

00 · The honest money answer

Is the invoice integrated?

Yes — Bluefin, not Charm. Charm has no invoice API. We cannot click Charm for you. When PayConex Account ID + Hosted Form ID are on the server, Ryan Approve emails and texts a Bluefin pay page. They do not type a card on tryregenrx.com.

Orders → Send pay link resends it. Type a dollar amount first when there is no catalog SKU (most sexual-health tickets). Charm is backup for credits, $1 tests, and in-chair charts.

What else is integrated

  • Square $49 phone telehealth — they book and pay Square. Confirmation comes from Square.
  • FormuConnect “Send to Formulation” — after the Bluefin invoice posts and the ticket has a SKU, one tap charges the clinic Formulation account (wholesale) and forwards the Rx. That is not the patient invoice.
  • In-chair Square Terminal — tap / dip / swipe still allowed if they are at 74 W. Washington.

What is blocked on purpose: Square pay links, texts, and card-on-file for prescription products. Square’s terms do not allow remote RX cards. The old “RX invoices” button on our admin that built a Square link is disabled. Do not work around it.

02 · Two doors (pick one)

Door A — Talk first (preferred)

  1. They book tryregenrx.com/consult — Ryan Kent phone visit, $49, 15 minutes. Square takes the card.
  2. They get Square’s confirmation (email/SMS) with the appointment time. Ryan calls them.
  3. If he does not prescribe: they paid for the visit. No therapy refund.
  4. If he does prescribe: send them tryregenrx.com/start if intake is not done. Credit the $49 on the therapy invoice (type the net amount on Orders, or apply it in Charm) and paste the Square receipt id in the chart.

Door B — Request first

  1. They complete tryregenrx.com/start (~8 min). Illinois, 21+. GORGEOUS20 = 20% off first medication order, shipping excluded, Ryan still decides.
  2. They do not pay for medication on the website.
  3. Ryan reviews at tryregenrx.com/ops → Today → Needs action → Chart.
  4. If yes: Approve sends the Bluefin pay link. If no: stop. No invoice.

Out of state: stop. We do not work around Illinois.

04 · How to send the invoice (automatic Bluefin · Charm backup)

Normal path: Ryan Approves. The patient is emailed and texted. Resend on Orders → Send pay link. Type dollars first if there is no catalog SKU.

Charm backup — sign in: https://accounts.charmtracker.com/signin

  1. Open the patient → Billing → + Invoice.
  2. Clear Insurance / Payer. Self-pay. If insurance is on the invoice, Patient Responsibility stays $0 and Bluefin will not charge.
  3. Add the product/visit row. Click Charge and type real dollars (example 299.00). Quantity at least 1. Tab out.
  4. Bottom must show Total / Balance Due in dollars — not $0. If it is $0, Bluefin has nothing to take.
  5. Apply GORGEOUS20 (20% product only) or the $49 consult credit here. There is no website coupon for this.
  6. Send Invoice → Send Payment Link (email and/or SMS). Pick the Hello Gorgeous / REGEN Bluefin beneficiary.
  7. Wait until Charm shows the payment. Paste the Charm receipt id in the chart.

In the chair: Charm → Add Payment → Card → Use Payment Gateway → Key-In or reader. Or Square Terminal for RX only if they are physically in Oswego.

Stuck $0: see the staff pay kit at /staff/protocols/guides/Charm-Bluefin-Take-a-Payment.html.

05 · Ryan’s clinical queue

  1. Open tryregenrx.com/ops (or /regen/ops). Today → Needs action.
  2. Teal Review opens the chart. Chart URL: /ops/patients/chart?email= — do not put a dotted email in the path.
  3. Thin history = labs or video. Do not guess.
  4. Four boxes must be true before green Approve: history reviewed, contraindications considered, telehealth appropriate, “I am Ryan Kent and this is my decision.”
  5. Write a note a lawyer could read in two years.
  6. needs_video and needs_labs stay in Needs action until Approve or Decline. That is not a bug.
  7. Only Ryan (or a covering Illinois-licensed prescriber Danielle named in writing) clicks Approve.

If the program has no safe default SKU (HRT, men’s performance), Ryan picks the Formulation SKU on the ticket before anyone sends it.

06 · Send the Rx (Formulation / FormuConnect)

Default pharmacy is Formulation Rx. The API is on. Chelsea’s key works. Ryan’s NPI 1265021919 goes on the payload. Send to Formulation charges the clinic account (card on file / net terms) and forwards the Rx to PioneerRx. It does not charge the patient.

  1. Confirm Charm (or Terminal) payment has posted.
  2. Confirm intake has name, DOB, Female or Male, street, city, ZIP, phone. Missing any of those stops the send.
  3. Open hellogorgeousmedspa.com/admin/rx/regen-orders → that order.
  4. Ryan has approved. Ticket says SKU ready.
  5. Tap pink Send to Formulation — charges Formulation.
  6. Pharmacy id writes back on the order. Do not tap it twice. A second tap will not re-charge if the receipt is already saved.

Backup: Copy FormuConnect ticket → paste in portal.formuconnect.com → then Mark FormuConnect order placed (manual).

BoomRx only

BPC/TB and blends Formulation does not carry. Paste in the BoomRx portal. FormuConnect cannot take those SKUs. Patients still hear “licensed compounding pharmacy.” Never say BoomRx, Wolverine, KLOW, or ASCEND to a client.

Charm eRx (if Ryan writes inside Charm) is a separate door into the pharmacy. Do not double-send the same fill through FormuConnect unless you meant to.

07 · After it ships

  1. When Formulation has tracking, put it on the order (carrier + number).
  2. One patient message: “Your medication left the pharmacy. Tracking is in your account.”
  3. Refills are another clinical review — not an automatic monthly charge.

08 · Decline and refunds

  • Ryan declines before a Charm charge: do not invoice.
  • They already paid Charm and he declines before the Rx is placed: refund that medication charge the same business day in Charm (Billing → that payment → refund). Paste the Charm receipt id in the chart.
  • There is no refund button in /ops/payments. Do not open Stripe.
  • After compounded / shipped: no vial refund. A licensed Illinois clinician reviews whether they continue.
  • $49 Square visit: if he does not prescribe, the visit fee stays.
