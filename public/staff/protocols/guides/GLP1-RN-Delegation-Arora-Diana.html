5. How Diana injects

  • SQ only. Abdomen (≥2 in from navel), thigh, or back of upper arm. Rotate. Skip bruised / scarred / infected skin.
  • Alcohol stopper + skin; dry. Insulin syringe or pharmacy needle. 90° into pinched SQ tissue unless the order says otherwise.
  • Draw the ordered mg from the labeled concentration. Unclear math = no injection.
  • No recapping. Sharps container. Fridge 36–46°F; never freeze; never past BUD.
  • First dose of a molecule (or any prior allergy concern): observe 15 minutes.
  • Teach: smaller meals, protein, call clinic or 911 for red-flag pain / cannot keep fluids / swelling or trouble breathing.

6. Hard stops — do not inject, call Dr. Arora

Pregnant / trying · MTC or MEN 2 newly reported · prior GLP-1 anaphylaxis · severe abdominal pain, intractable vomiting, jaundice, suspected pancreatitis today · patient wants a different dose than the order · unlabeled / expired / frozen / patient-brought vial · under 21 · not in Illinois · order expired. Breastfeeding: inject only if the chart has an explicit clinician yes for this patient.

7. Anaphylaxis

911 first. Then IM epinephrine 0.3 mg (1:1000 = 0.3 mL, or adult autoinjector) anterolateral thigh. Repeat once in 5–15 min if still in anaphylaxis and EMS not in control — only if Dr. Arora initials:
