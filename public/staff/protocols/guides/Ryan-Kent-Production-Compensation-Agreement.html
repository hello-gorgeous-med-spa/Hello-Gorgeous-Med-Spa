2. What this engagement is — and is not

This is
  • 1099-NEC independent contractor
  • Compensation only for personally performed professional services
  • Weekly Square Payroll contractor payout
  • Contractor pays own taxes; W-9 on file
This is not
  • Employment, partnership, equity, or profit share
  • A salary, hourly wage, or monthly oversight stipend
  • A bounty per prescription written
  • Ownership of patients, charts, or the brand

5. Clinical independence (non-negotiable)

Production pay never requires a prescription or treatment. Decline, stop, or choose a lower-cost indicated option when that is the right medicine. Practice will not set a prescription quota or “units written” target. Unnecessary or unsafe prescribing is immediate cause for termination. This is pay for personally performed professional services — not fee-splitting with an unlicensed person, not a kickback, not an equity draw.

6. Patients and records stay with the Practice

  • Patients, leads, charts, photos, pharmacy relationships, and contact lists are Practice property. Ryan is treating clinician / assignee only.
  • No bulk export of the patient list, photo library, or marketing list without owner-approved, logged export.
  • On termination or demand: return keys, badges, devices; access is cut the same day.
  • Practice marks, RE GEN branding, and patient photos are not for a competing venture.

7. Confidentiality & HIPAA

Contractor complies with HIPAA, HITECH, Illinois confidentiality law, and Practice privacy policy. PHI is used only to treat and operate. Wholesale pricing, pay formulas, protocols, and vendor terms remain confidential during and after this engagement.

8. Non-solicitation — 18 months after end

During the term and for 18 months after, Contractor will not, using Practice lists or knowledge:

  • solicit or divert Practice patients Contractor treated or prescribed for, to a competing aesthetic, GLP-1, peptide, or HRT practice;
  • solicit Practice employees or contractors to leave;
  • falsely disparage the Practice, owner, Medical Director, or staff to divert patients.

Does not bar a patient who independently seeks Contractor without solicitation, or truthful board / court / insurer responses. A court may reform this clause to the maximum Illinois allows.

9. Licensure & malpractice

  • Keep unrestricted Illinois APRN license, FNP-BC, NPI, DEA if prescribing controlled substances, BLS, and any collaborative agreement Illinois requires with the serving Medical Director.
  • Written notice to Practice within 24 hours of license restriction, board complaint, malpractice claim, DEA issue, or federal exclusion.
  • Malpractice covering aesthetics and prescribing: at least $1,000,000 / $3,000,000 (or carrier-required limits). Name Hello Gorgeous, P.C. additional insured / certificate holder where the policy allows. Lapse = immediate suspension of services and pay.

10. Indemnity (short)

Contractor indemnifies Practice, Danielle Alcala-Glazier, Hello Gorgeous, P.C., staff, and the Medical Director for Contractor’s professional negligence, unauthorized prescribing, HIPAA breaches by Contractor, and Contractor tax-misclassification claims caused by Contractor. Practice indemnifies Contractor for premises / device / other-staff claims not directed by Contractor, except to the extent Contractor caused them.
