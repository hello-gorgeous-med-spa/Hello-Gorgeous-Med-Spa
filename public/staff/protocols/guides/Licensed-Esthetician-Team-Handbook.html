1. Who we are

Hello Gorgeous Med Spa is a medical aesthetics practice at 74 W. Washington Street, Oswego, IL 60543. Phone (630) 636-6193. Clients come for a natural, not-overdone look. We charge what we do. We chart what we do.

Owner: Danielle Alcala. Floor, schedule, and pay questions go to Danielle. Clinical / device protocols that require a medical director follow Dr. Mukesh Arora, MD and covering Illinois clinicians.

2. Your role

You are engaged as a Licensed Esthetician for esthetics and lash services, including:

  • Lash perm / lift and tint
  • Lash lamination and brow lamination
  • Classic / full-set lash extensions and fills
  • HydraFacial and dermaplaning facials (and other facials on our menu you are trained to perform)
  • Consults, retail, sanitation, charting, and rebooking
Laser is not automatic. You do not operate laser or energy devices until you complete clinic training, hold any required certification, and Danielle (and, where required, the medical director) signs you off in writing. Until then, you do not turn the device on a paying client.

3. 1099 · part-time · 90 days

You are a 1099 independent contractor, not a W-2 employee. You set aside money for taxes. The Practice does not withhold income tax, Social Security, or Medicare, and does not offer employee health insurance. Your agreed service schedule is on your offer letter (Danielle fills the hours). This is a part-time engagement.

The first 90 days from your start date are an introductory review period. We will sit down on or about day 90 with the written review form. Either party may end this engagement with written notice during or after those 90 days.

Be ready 10 minutes before your first client. Text or call Danielle if you will be late or out. A no-show on your side without notice can end the engagement. Time off with 5 days’ notice to provider@hellogorgeousmedspa.com (unpaid). Sick / emergency — tell her as soon as you can.

4. Your book

  • Work the repeating hours on your offer. You may request extra bookable time in Square if Danielle agrees.
  • Treat, charge, rebook before they stand up, send the Google review link.
  • If a service needs more time than the slot, book a second slot and charge for the work. Do not run into the next client.
  • After a 14-day ramp, repeating empty blocks may be shortened or removed with 7 days’ notice.

5. Pay

Weekly Square contractor payout after your W-9 is on file. Rates are on your signed offer (Danielle fills them in). Tips on your Square profile are yours unless the offer says otherwise. Ask Danielle within 7 days if a number looks wrong.

Perk — services for you. When the book allows (not a paying client’s slot), you may use agreed non-consumable facial / device time on yourself if Danielle approves that week. Book the room on Square. Consumables (lash trays, tint, peel, tips, serums) are not included unless she says yes.

6. Safety, consent, sanitation

  • Consent signed before you treat. Chart the same day.
  • Patch-test / allergy questions for tint, perm, lamination, and peel as our protocols require.
  • Eye safety for lash work. Never tint lashes or brows against a contraindication on the consent.
  • Illinois esthetician license stays current. We verify on IDFPR. If it lapses, you stop treating that day.
  • Clean, disinfect, and reset the room to our standard between clients. Tools sterilized or discarded as required.
  • Unexpected reaction: stop, follow protocol, get Danielle / covering clinician. Do not “wait and see.”

7. HIPAA, phones, photos

  • No client faces on your personal camera roll “for fun.”
  • No names in group chats or Instagram DMs.
  • No posting a client without a signed media release.
  • Lock the screen. Do not discuss one client in front of another.

8. Look and conduct

  • Clean, polished, on-brand. Hair off the face when treating. Nails safe for close eye work.
  • No arguing prices at the front. No talking down the last provider. No drama on the floor.
  • Phone: clients first. Personal scrolling between faces, not in front of them.

9. Product and pricing

Published menu or owner-approved quote. What you use is what you charge. Unbilled product can come off your next deposit at retail. Written owner approval is the only exception, and it happens before the service.

10. Clients and loyalty

Clients, charts, before/afters, and price lists stay here. You do not take our book with you. During this engagement you do not book or treat for another med spa during Hello Gorgeous scheduled hours. Ask in writing if you think something is a gray area.
