2. At-will contractor

This is
  • 1099-NEC · at will · either party may end it in writing any time
  • Clinical lead for RE GEN RX (not ownership)
  • Clinic time as the book grows — you are building toward full-time clinic work
  • Telehealth bookable online
  • Weekly Square 1099 payout · you handle taxes · W-9 on file
This is not
  • Employment, partnership, equity, or a salary
  • A guaranteed full-time schedule or minimum draw
  • Staff becoming prescribers
  • Ownership of patients, charts, or the brand
  • A bounty per prescription written

Email notice is enough: provider@hellogorgeousmedspa.com. After notice, stay reasonably available 7 days for urgent questions on open charts.

3. Protocol pathway — your license, our charting

You adopt written protocols. That is how the Practice may act on your license, under your authority. Trained staff may complete intake, chart, prepare the matching order, and call in / transmit the prescription. They call you when needed.

They must stop and reach you before transmitting if the case is outside protocol, first-time or high-risk, a red-flag / adverse event, the pharmacy questions it, or they are unsure. Nothing outside protocol ships until you (or a covering licensed prescriber) approve. You publish a reachable number and coverage hours. The owner and unlicensed staff are never the prescriber.

Examples. Quantum collected $3,900, COGS $400 → NET $3,500 → $1,750. Telehealth consult $99, no COGS → $19.80. Tirzepatide month $399, pharmacy $120 → after cost $279 → $55.80. Toxin $400 you inject → $80. You decline the chart → $0.

5. Clinic + online telehealth

We will grow your in-clinic book toward full-time as demand allows. Hours stay at-will — not a guarantee. Telehealth can be scheduled online. Whatever you prescribe on those visits pays Bucket R when we collect.

6. Clinical independence

You treat and prescribe only when it is right for that patient. Pay never buys a yes. We may audit charts and protocol use. Unsafe or unnecessary prescribing, or a protocol breach, ends this engagement immediately.

7. License and malpractice

You keep an unrestricted Illinois APRN, FNP-BC, NPI, DEA if you will prescribe controlled substances, BLS, and any required collaborative paper with Dr. Arora. Tell us within 24 hours if anything hits your license, DEA, or board. Malpractice covering aesthetics and prescribing, $1M / $3M, Hello Gorgeous, P.C. on the certificate. If it lapses, you stop and pay stops.

8. Patients and HIPAA

Patients, charts, photos, and pharmacy relationships stay with the Practice. Chart in our systems. Return keys and access the same day this ends. HIPAA applies. No exporting the book.

9. Non-solicit

During this engagement and for 18 months after, you do not use our lists or knowledge to pull patients you treated or prescribed for, or our staff, to a competing aesthetic / GLP-1 / peptide / HRT practice. A patient who finds you on their own is not a breach.

10. Ending it

At will — either of us, any time, in writing. Immediate if license or malpractice lapses, fraud, HIPAA breach, unsafe prescribing, or you present as owner. Final earned pay (minus clawbacks) within two pay cycles after we close attribution.
