This letter is for you. It is not an internal memo. Please read it with your offer letter and team handbook. Those two documents have the details you sign. This one is the hello.

What you are walking into

Hello Gorgeous is a medical aesthetics practice in downtown Oswego. Clients come for a natural, confidence-first result — not an overdone look. We also run RE GEN RX, our peptide and weight-loss door. You will consult, educate, and sell those programs. You will not prescribe. Our nurse practitioner and medical director write the orders. You get paid when you close the sale and we collect.

How we will work together at the start

Your weekday hours are still open on purpose. You and I will sit down and build a week that covers some mornings, some afternoons, and some evenings so clients can actually get in. You commit to at least 25 hours a week, including every other Saturday. You will have your own Square booking access. Our goal is to keep you busy. You also have the option to open extra hours you want to work.

What we will teach you

You will learn our device and advanced aesthetic path, paid at your hourly rate while you are on site training:

  • Laser treatments
  • Solaria CO₂
  • Morpheus8
  • Microneedling
  • Advanced techniques: PDRN, baby Botox, and exosomes

You will also consult, recommend the next right treatment, and get paid on the peptides and weight-loss programs you sell.

How you are paid

You are a 1099 independent contractor. You are paid $50 per hour for the hours you actually work here, plus your tips. If any clock hour collects more than $800 on your appointments, that hour is automatically $100 — a $50 production bonus on the $50 floor. You also earn 10% of collected peptide and weight-loss sales you personally close.

The offer letter writes the math so there is no surprise on payday.

A perk of this role: you may use our non-consumable machines on yourself, free of charge, when the book allows — Solaria, laser hair, waxing, microneedling, HydraFacials, and similar. Consumable product is not included unless I approve. Time off is granted with 5 days’ notice to provider@hellogorgeousmedspa.com.

I am excited to have you on this floor. Bring your clinical eye, your consult style, and your Instagram voice (@aestheticsby.kristina). We will give you the devices, the book, and the support to get busy.

Welcome to the team, Kristina. Let’s make this a beautiful chapter — for you and for every client who sits with you.
