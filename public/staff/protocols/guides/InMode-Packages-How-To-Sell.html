90-second framework

  1. Mirror their goal (jowls, scars, post-GLP skin, tone).
  2. Name the tech — InMode verified · Burst + Deep · Solaria · NP-directed.
  3. Why a series — collagen builds over months; one visit rarely finishes it.
  4. Put the package — Transformation (face) or Ultimate (body).
  5. Show the math once — then proposal + deposit or Cherry.

Scripts — say this

Face → Transformation

Say: “For face tightening and texture we use Morpheus8 Burst — real InMode RF, usually a series of three about six weeks apart. If you also want the surface renewed — tone, sun damage, that new-skin look — we add one Solaria CO₂ full face. That’s our Transformation Package at $2,400 — instead of roughly thirty-three hundred booking them separate.”

Body → Ultimate

Say: “Body skin is different than face. The Ultimate Package is $3,000 for a Morpheus8 series on two body areas. Doing those one-off is thousands more — the package is how we finish the series.”

Morpheus8 3-pack only

Say: “If we’re focused on one zone and don’t need laser yet, the three-session Morpheus8 package at $1,999 is the cleanest way to commit to remodeling.”

Solaria alone

Say: “Solaria is our fractional CO₂ — resurfacing for wrinkles, acne scars, sun damage, tone. Full face is $899 (BOGO area when the special is on). Downtime is real — we map your calendar. If you also have laxity, we usually put Morpheus8 underneath and Solaria on top — that’s Transformation.”

Why both (Trifecta line)

Say: “Morpheus8 remodels under the skin. Solaria refreshes the surface. Doing only one leaves half the story unfinished for a lot of clients who want that full renewed look.”

Close

Say: “I’ll put this on a treatment proposal you can open on your phone. We can take a deposit today on Square — or apply with Cherry if monthly feels better.”

Do / don’t

Do

Use package names · show math once · always send a proposal · be honest about Solaria downtime · upsell exosomes + vitamins after yes.

Don’t

Guarantee outcomes · bash competitors by name · leave only a verbal quote · undersell downtime · forget InMode verified + NP trust line.
