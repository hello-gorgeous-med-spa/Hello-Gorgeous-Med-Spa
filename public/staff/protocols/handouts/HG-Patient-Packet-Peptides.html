Consent

A. General treatment consent

I authorize Hello Gorgeous Med Spa / Hello Gorgeous RX™ and Ryan Kent, FNP-BC to evaluate me for peptide therapy and related wellness treatments. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

B. HIPAA

I acknowledge use/disclosure of my health information for treatment, payment, and operations as allowed by HIPAA.

C. Peptide therapy informed consent

  • Peptide protocols are individualized and may include compounded medications.
  • Many compounded peptides are not FDA-approved for the wellness uses discussed; some uses may be off-label.
  • Risks may include injection-site reactions, flushing, headache, nausea, water retention, mood/libido changes (protocol-dependent), allergic reaction, and unknown long-term risks for some compounds.
  • This is not a substitute for primary care, oncology care, or emergency care.
  • A telehealth or in-person NP visit is required before prescription or refill approval.
  • I will disclose all supplements, hormones, and prior gray-market peptide use.
  • I will stop and call for concerning symptoms; I will not adjust dosing on my own.

E. Financial

I understand consult fees, medication, shipping, and follow-ups may be billed separately. Charges must match what is dispensed or ordered.
