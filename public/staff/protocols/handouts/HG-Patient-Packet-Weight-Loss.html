C. Medical weight loss / GLP-1 informed consent

May include: Semaglutide (Ozempic®/Wegovy®), Tirzepatide (Mounjaro®/Zepbound®), compounded equivalents when prescribed.

  • Works by reducing appetite, slowing stomach emptying, and affecting blood sugar.
  • Common: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, stomach pain, decreased appetite.
  • Serious (less common): pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, kidney issues, allergic reaction, thyroid tumors (boxed warning — animal studies).
  • Do not use with personal/family MTC or MEN 2; or if pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive without clearance.
  • Compounded products are not FDA-approved for all described uses; off-label use may apply.
  • Weekly injection · titration · follow-ups required · report severe symptoms immediately.
  • Weight may return if medication is stopped.
