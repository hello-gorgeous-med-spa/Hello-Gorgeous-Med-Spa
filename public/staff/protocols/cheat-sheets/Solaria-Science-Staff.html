Why CO₂ is the medical tool for resurfacing and texture

Say this once, then shut up and ask which photo they hate:
“Skin is water. This laser is absorbed by water, so it vaporizes tiny columns and leaves bridges of skin to heal from. That’s a planned medical wound — that’s why it actually changes the surface. Creams can’t do that. Morpheus8 is a different layer. This is the reset.”

The physics (know it; don’t lecture it)

FactStaff meaningClient line
10,600 nm CO₂ Peak absorption in water. Epidermis/dermis vaporize in the beam path (ablation). Non-ablative devices mostly heat. “It doesn’t just warm the skin. It removes a precise column.”
Fractional pattern Micro-ablative zones + untreated bridges. Re-epithelialization from bridges + adnexa (follicles, glands). Faster than 1990s full-field CO₂. “Not the old peel that took the whole face off. Tiny dots, healthy skin in between.”
Ablation + coagulated rim Vaporized core + thermal collar. Residual heat → fibroblast activity, collagen remodeling for months. Why CO₂ beats a purely “cold” polish for lines/texture. “The peel is week one. The collagen keeps working for months.”
RF-excited microbeam (Solaria / InMode) Device control of mJ, density, tip, pattern. QRG rows exist because energy and density are inverse. “We map depth to your downtime — not one setting for every face.”

Resurfacing vs texture — same laser, different job

ResurfacingTexture improvement
Biology Strip damaged stratum corneum / epidermis in columns so a new surface forms. Rhytids, crepe, photoaged field. Knock down peaks (pores, roughness, scar shoulders). Heat collar remodels valleys (collagen). Architecture, not just color.
Why CO₂ is chosen You need actual ablation of the surface. Topicals and RF that never open the epidermis cannot replace this layer. Scar and pore geometry sit deeper than a pigment dusting. QRG texture row is higher mJ than dyschromia for that reason.
Say “This is the new-skin pass.” “This is the sandpaper / pore / scar-surface pass. It asks more of the skin, so downtime has to match.”
Don’t say “CO₂ melts scars 80%.” “No downtime.” “Same as a chemical peel.” “Safer than surgery so it’s not medical.”

Tie it to the QRG in one breath

  • Dyschromia = color in the epidermis → low energy, can be denser. Pepper, don’t carve.
  • Resurfacing = new surface → mid energy band.
  • Texture = architecture → energy shifted up. Same densities as resurfacing on the card.
  • Neck = thinner, off-face → Micro, 5–15 mJ, one pass. Heat + ablation here is less forgiving.

Why we call it medical (use this when they want a “facial laser”)

  • Open wound pattern on purpose → infection, PIH, scarring, HSV flare are real.
  • Plume can carry tissue. Smoke evacuator + eyewear / shields are not theater.
  • Candidacy (isotretinoin, pregnancy, tan, event date) is the science, not red tape.
  • If they want zero downtime, they want a different tool — say that and offer Morpheus8 or a later date. Do not sell Heavy texture into a wedding.
