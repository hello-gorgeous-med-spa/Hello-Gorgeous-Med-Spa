Application protocol — what to pick, and why

One primary application per zone per visit unless the clinician documents a second, gentler pass for a different goal (example: dyschromia on temples, texture on cheeks). Do not average two applications into one “hot and dense” setting. Higher density = lower energy. Lower density = higher energy. Off-face (neck, chest) = turn everything down.

Step 1 — What is the #1 thing they hate in this zone?

Skin resurfacing

IF lines, crepe, “I want new skin,” overall quality, photo-aged field without the main complaint being pits or brown.

THEN Resurfacing row. This is the global refresh. Energy sits below texture.

Not the pigment pass. Not the scar-roughness pass.

Texture improvement

IF pores, sandpaper, acne-scar surface, roughness they can feel with a fingertip.

THEN Texture row. Same densities as resurfacing, energy shifted up (light starts 30 mJ, heavy to 70 mJ).

This is the more aggressive quality pass. Match downtime or don’t pick Heavy.

Dyschromia

IF brown, blotch, sun spots, mottled tone — color more than lines.

THEN Dyschromia row. Much lower energy (5–25 mJ), higher density allowed. You are peppering color, not carving columns for wrinkles.

Melasma is not automatically this row — heat can flare it. Stop and rethink.

Neck

IF the zone is neck (or you are treating chest as off-face).

THEN Neck row only. Micro tip. Light 5–15 mJ. One pass. Do not copy the face program onto the neck.

Even if the face is Heavy texture, neck stays the Neck line.

Step 3 — Micro vs Macro vs Face tip (this is anatomy, not the complaint)

Tip QRG job Use it on Do not use it to…
Micro Standard fractional ablation · face and neck · smaller spots Default. All neck. Most face work. Any time you want finer columns. Skip it on neck so you can “cover faster” with Macro.
Macro Larger treatment areas / more extensive ablation · slightly larger spots than Micro · higher % density on the chart Broad fields: cheeks, forehead, jaw, full-face real estate when you need coverage. Eyelids, lips, sides of nose, or neck. Macro is not “stronger resurfacing” — it is a bigger spot.
Face Same ablation as Micro · built for tight geometry Nose, periorbital, perioral — hard-to-reach corners. Paint the whole cheek with Face tip “because it sounds fancier.”

Typical full-face map: Macro on cheeks/forehead → Face tip around eyes, lips, nose → Micro (or Face) at hairline/edges as needed → switch to Neck row + Micro if you continue under the jaw. Scanner for all of this. Surgical handpiece is lesion incision/excision only (4–10 W, 90 ms, 10 PPS) — not a menu add-on.

Mode & pattern (internal)

  • Repeat — extra pass on the same scanned area. QRG: typically deep fractional. That is how Light gets “1 to 2” passes. Medium/Heavy stay 1 unless clinician documents why.
  • Continuous — keep scanning without lifting the footswitch. Do not narrate the pedal to the guest.
  • Random vs sequential — layout of the dots. Sequential = line by line. Random = less patterned look. Pick for even coverage, not as a sales word.

Room order (every Solaria)

  1. Candidate + downtime + photos.
  2. Write the plan: application + tip + light/med/heavy + zones before the laser comes on.
  3. Test spot when new patient, PIH risk, or any setting change. Several energies, smallest spot that still shows the expected response. Wait for healing if the QRG “several days prior” applies — don’t skip because the slot is full.
  4. Numbing ≤ 45–60 min. Sensitive skin: extra caution. Reaction → stop.
  5. Scanner + smoke evacuator + eyewear / shields.
  6. Start at the low end of the chosen band. Watch tissue. Energy first, then density.
  7. Wanted look: erythema, edema, stippled gray. Not char, not confluent white sheet.
  8. Do not debride eschar. It is the dressing.
  9. Teach aftercare out loud. Book follow-up photos.

Chart this or don’t fire

  • Primary application per zone
  • Tip (Micro / Macro / Face)
  • Band (L/M/H) and actual mJ / density used
  • Passes / Repeat yes-no
  • Test spot result
  • Neck/chest called out separately
Hard stops: Do not pick Texture Heavy because the special is $599. Do not run high mJ and high density together. Do not put Macro or face-Heavy on the neck. Do not stack Resurfacing Heavy + Texture Heavy on one zone. Do not treat melasma as “just dyschromia.” Do not treat the same day as consult if prep (antiviral, no tan, ride home) is missing.
