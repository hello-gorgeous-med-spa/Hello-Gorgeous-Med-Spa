What is it?Fractional CO₂ — tiny columns, untreated bridges, faster heal than old full-field. Medical resurfacing, not a facial.“No downtime laser.” “Lunchtime peel.”
What will it fix?Name their photo: lines / sun / texture / scars / neck. Map the tool to that. Neck is gentler on purpose.“It fixes everything.” Guaranteed % better.
Downtime?Light 5–7 makeup / moderate ~7–10 / deeper 2+ weeks. Ask their event date first.“You’ll be fine for Saturday.”
When do I see it?Peel week one. Remodeling 3–6 months. Results vary.“You’ll look like Michelle next week.”
How many?One can be enough. Series if scars/sun need it, months apart. Clinician sequences it.Selling a 6-pack before you’ve seen them heal.
Pain?Numb 30–45 min. Heat + prickle. Sunburn after. Reaction to numb = stop.“You won’t feel a thing.”
Morpheus instead?Surface vs under-the-surface. Pick from the photo. Trifecta if they need both — after the yes on today’s tool.Dumping on Morpheus or another spa.
Price?Face $599 fall + serum through 10/31. Neck/chest/eyes quoted today because they’re slower and gentler.“We’re the cheapest CO₂.” Hide extra zones.
Neck/chest?Yes, reduced settings off the face. Safety, not upsell. Quote it.Same settings as cheeks.
Under eyes?Candidate + small tip + shields. Consult map. Not a walk-in add-on.“Easy add for $99.”
Makeup / gym / sun?Closed skin first. SPF 50+ and a hat. Give a date from their depth.“Just wear mineral tomorrow.”
Cold soresThank them for telling you. Antiviral 1–2 days prior if indicated.Skipping history to keep the appointment.
AccutanePause. Recent isotretinoin = wait. Don’t tell them to stop a Rx.“It’s been 2 months, you’re fine.”
Darker skinPIH risk is real. Type, counsel, maybe test-spot or different plan.“CO₂ isn’t for your skin” as a blanket. Or “safe for all Fitzpatricks.”
MelasmaHeat can flare it. May not be first-line. Honest redirect.“This will erase melasma.”
Active acneWait if it’s hot/infected. Scars after the fire is out.Laser as an acne cure.
PregnantNo Solaria in pregnancy. Breastfeeding = clinician.“Just skip the numbing.”
Filler / BotoxDates please. Often keep toxin; wait after filler on that zone.Guessing last syringe date.
Will I look scary?Red → bronze → flake. Don’t pick. That’s healing, not a hidden burn.Filtered after-photos as “day 2.”
Test spot?Yes when history/skin/settings call for it. Tiny test beats a full-face surprise.Skipping a test after you changed parameters.
Same day?Only if candidate + downtime + prep + schedule. Else book the right date.Same-day deep CO₂ before a wedding.
Lasts forever?You still age. SPF is the maintenance. We don’t sell permanent.“Once and done for life.”
